## Software engineering for systems that need to work in the real world

I am a backend software engineer with around five years of professional experience, working primarily with **Java and Spring**. My background includes financial systems, REST APIs, messaging, cross-domain integrations, legacy applications, and production environments.

I enjoy turning complex problems into flows that are understandable, testable, and safe to evolve — without adding architecture where clarity would do a better job.

## How I approach engineering

- The domain comes before the framework.
- Simplicity is a technical decision, not a lack of ambition.
- Critical changes need observable validation and a way back.
- Sustainable code leaves useful context for the next person.
